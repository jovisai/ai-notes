# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers and engineering leaders reading practical guides and reflective essays about AI agents, software engineering, and engineering management, on desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

AI Agent Engineering Notes is Muthu Krishnan's personal publication. Help readers understand technical ideas and consider how they affect their work through clear, sustained reading.

## Brand Personality

Thoughtful, practical, understated. Preserve the existing publication identity and its Crimson Pro and Libre Franklin pairing.

## Anti-references

Avoid promotional landing-page styling, decorative typography, dense text, and visual treatments that distract from the argument.

## Design Principles

- Let the writing lead.
- Make long passages comfortable to read.
- Distinguish article headings from navigation and supporting metadata.
- Preserve the established identity when refining individual articles.
- Make changes work on both narrow screens and desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable text, clear contrast, keyboard access, and layouts that accommodate 200% zoom. No additional audience-specific requirements have been identified.
